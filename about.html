Made slowly,
worn for years.

Verso began in 2019 as a three-person studio with one question: what remains when trend is removed?

Our philosophy is restraint. Each piece is cut from natural fibres by mills we visit, finished by makers we know by name, and designed to outlast the season it launches in. Our mission is a wardrobe of fewer, better things.

Craftsmanship

Hand-finished seams, corozo buttons, and linings that are repaired, not replaced. Every garment ships with a lifetime mending service.

Sustainability

Ninety percent of our fabrics are certified organic, recycled, or deadstock. We publish our supplier list and our emissions each year, including where we fall short.

Honest materials

Wool, linen, leather, cashmere.

Fair making

Audited workshops, living wages.

Long life

Repairs for the life of the piece.
